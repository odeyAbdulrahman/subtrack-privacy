# SubTrack

SubTrack is a local-first Android application for tracking subscriptions, invoices, expenses, renewals, and payment activity.

## Features

- Track subscriptions, trials, renewals, invoices, and expenses.
- Review daily, weekly, monthly, and calendar-year spending.
- Optionally scan supported financial SMS notifications on Android.
- Review detected transactions before they affect your records.
- Keep supported app data on your device.

Optional Gmail receipt scanning may be available in verified releases. When available, it requires the user's explicit read-only Gmail permission and is used only to identify relevant subscription, invoice, and payment messages for review.

SubTrack does not move money, execute payments, provide loans, or offer financial advice.
