# SubTrack Privacy Policy

Effective date: 30 August 2026

SubTrack is a local-first application for tracking subscriptions, invoices,
expenses, and payment activity. This policy explains how the app handles data.

## Data stored on your device

SubTrack stores the information you enter or confirm, including subscriptions,
invoices, expenses, payment history, preferences, and optional profile details,
in a local database on your device. This information is not sent to the
developer.

You can delete individual records or erase all local application data from the
privacy controls in the app. Uninstalling the app also removes its local data,
subject to your device's operating-system behaviour.

## SMS access

On supported Android devices, SubTrack may request read-only access to SMS
messages when you choose to scan for financial transaction notifications. The
app uses this access to identify possible debit, refund, invoice-payment, and
subscription events for your review.

SMS scanning is optional. Message processing occurs on the device. SubTrack
does not upload SMS messages to the developer, does not store complete message
bodies, and excludes authentication and one-time-password messages. A detected
transaction is not included in expense analytics until you confirm it.

## Notifications and device information

SubTrack may schedule local notifications for reminders you configure. These
notifications are generated on your device. The app does not use an advertising
identifier and does not contain advertising in this release.

## Data sharing and sale

The developer does not receive, sell, or share your personal data. Gmail and
Gemini integrations are not available in this release.

## Security

SubTrack uses local application storage for its records and settings. No method
of storage is completely risk-free, so you should keep your device protected
and install operating-system security updates.

## Children's privacy

SubTrack is intended for adults and is not directed to children.

## Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the app's features or data practices change. The
effective date at the top of this page will be updated when material changes are
made.
